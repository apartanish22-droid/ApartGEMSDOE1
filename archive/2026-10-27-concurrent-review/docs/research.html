01 · MODERATE COST

Dual-step persistence

Look for consistent, co-oriented magnetic and gravity steps at multiple scales. Mimics include lithologic contacts and survey artifacts. A hash-pinned third-party feature mirror was restored; it is not organizer-authenticated.

02 · HIGH COST

Multi-channel drainage disruption

Test repeated stream deflections and slope breaks against an independent geophysical edge. Erosion, roads, lithology and lava dams can imitate this signal; suitable DEM coverage is not downloaded.

03 · MODERATE / HIGH COST

Hydrothermal disequilibrium transects

Contrast spring/well chemistry across lineaments against matched nonthermal sites. Fluid mixing and biased sampling are major confounders. The official archive metadata is public; its bytes were not fetched here.

04 · MODERATE COST

Conductance-depth discordance

Compare aligned conductivity and depth-to-base gradients with the regional gravity trend. Clay, lake deposits and inversion artifacts can mimic a fault; the depth-band description needs clarification.
