03 / SUBMISSION PLAYBOOK

When a candidate is eventually cleared

These are future instructions, not permission to submit anything today. No manual visual inspection is required by this project.

01

Pass the gates

A lane, leakage canaries, blocked holdout win, complete registry checks, exact-byte validation and a separate selector approval must all be recorded.

02

Download the cleared file

Only a future approved single-band GeoTIFF will be offered here with its exact SHA-256, unique name and note of at most 140 characters.

03

Use the contest form

On DrivenData, open Submit → Make new submission, choose the cleared file and paste its note. Check the current limit displayed on the form.

04

Keep the receipt

Record the organizer's submission-page receipt against the file hash. Choose one final submission for both prize rounds before the deadline.
