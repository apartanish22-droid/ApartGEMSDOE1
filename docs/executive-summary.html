Within three pixels of one registered calibration lattice, even if every uncovered cell were used. The user-specified stop threshold is 70%.

What the bound does—and does not—say

The reference covers 5,160,724 of 5,167,373 footprint cells within three pixels, leaving 6,649 uncovered. For 37,654 distinct dots, at least 31,005 must overlap. This does not prove that every smaller raster is impossible, or that a candidate is unique against the rest of the registry. Moving dots into leftover holes merely to defeat a gate is not a geological hypothesis.

Champion forensics

What could explain the reported result?

The inspected GEMSDOE32 B2 mask is exactly the GEMSDOE25 d=2.8 mask after deleting the 6,436 positive pixels within two pixels of the mapped catalogue; it adds none. GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical positive masks despite different file bytes. These are measured relationships, not proof of a score.

The official distance-weighted Tversky metric gives partial spatial credit within 300 m. Removing redundant or low-yield mass can reduce false-positive penalties, while removing dots near a genuine hidden fault can also sacrifice true-positive coverage. The unknown hidden labels, the exact live scoring mask and a file-specific organizer receipt are not available here; therefore causality—and whether a new strategy will beat the reported result—cannot be established. A mapped-fault holdout is a screen, not a calibrated leaderboard prediction.

Research, not promises

Four candidate directions.

These are ranked qualitative hypotheses, not measured gains or an assigned method lane. Each needs a full novelty check and the specified leakage-safe holdout.

01

Cross-physics edge offsets

Compare signed spatial offsets of multiscale gravity and magnetic gradients. Lithological contacts and survey artifacts are competing explanations.

02

Seasonal thermal persistence

Test Landsat surface-temperature residuals alongside independent structure. Irrigation, soil moisture and solar heating can mimic heat.

03

Repeated drainage breaks

Compare related stream-profile discontinuities across independent catchments. Lithology, shorelines and grading are alternatives.

Submission guide

How to submit, once approved.

  1. Do not act on the current snapshot. Obtain an explicitly assigned lane, resolve the literal registry conflict, and run the shared buffered holdout, feature canaries and full uniqueness checks. Confirm the candidate beats the comparable best with the required uncertainty evidence.
  2. Validate the saved file automatically. Confirm single-band float32, matching template CRS, shape and transform, and finite values in [0,1] inside the footprint. Review the reported outside-footprint encoding; local checks do not prove portal acceptance.
  3. After the separate selector approves a weekly slot, use the enabled candidate download and its unique name and ≤140-character note. No download is enabled today.
  4. On DrivenData, open the competition, choose Submit → Make new submission, select the TIFF or a ZIP containing exactly one TIFF, paste the note and submit. The rules mention three per week, but DrivenData staff say the limit displayed on the competition website prevails; the team’s allowance is unknown. Choose one final entry for both prize rounds.
