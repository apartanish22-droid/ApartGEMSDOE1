The [official distance-weighted Tversky formula](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#performance-metric) gives each truth pixel the **maximum** triangular-kernel credit of nearby predictions. Overlapping dots can therefore add little new credit while increasing false-positive mass. Pruning and spacing are plausible reasons for a change, but the hidden labels and artifact-score receipt are unavailable. 1. **Mirror is not official authentication.** The 19-band feature raster was independently assembled from five SHA-256-pinned GitHub chunks with the [unchanged 57GEMSDOE template](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/57GEMSDOE/blob/822a0ce5546c7b6749cdd82eb145b6b3e616603d/scripts/prepare_data.py). The [official data page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/data/) redirected to login. Pins establish mirror consistency, not a license or organizer origin.
2. **Sample inconsistency.** The organizer [problem page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/#submission-format) describes the sample as predicting total fault absence. The pinned mirror sample's positive support is identical to the mirrored labels (60,988 positives). It remains useful for a *conditional* alignment/footprint audit, but is not accepted as an authenticated official template without independent verification.
3. **Predicted-value error.** The official problem page requires one `float32` band, EPSG:32611, 100 m, matching bounds and values in [0,1], with null/NaN outside bounds. The user's portal previously rejected a file. The cause is unverified: possible out-of-range finite sentinels, NaNs or another input issue must not be asserted as fact. Future checks must read back the exact file, separately report raw non-finite/out-of-range cells inside and outside the valid footprint, and never silently clip invalid predictions. Organizer acceptance of a particular outside-footprint encoding is unverified.
4. **A wrapper can binarize probabilities.** At the pinned [GEMSDOE32 wrapper](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/1398109a23f04a2ace71c1bd2f527cfba87860b5/scripts/submission_writer.py#L59-L74), a positive `mask_or_probs` input becomes boolean (`> 0`) before writing. This may change continuous probability surfaces. The attributed [shared 57GEMSDOE writer](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/57GEMSDOE/blob/822a0ce5546c7b6749cdd82eb145b6b3e616603d/src/gems57/submission_writer.py) accepts continuous values, validates them, and does not silently repair them. No wrapper was used or private fork made. The finding was reported upstream in [GEMSDOE32 issue #51](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/issues/51); maintainers should clarify the binary-only contract or fix it centrally before continuous use. This checkout has no push permission to that repository.
5. **Quota and deadline.** The [organizer's quota clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/submission-amount-question/11553/2) says the limit displayed on the competition website prevails despite the rules' parenthetical. We cannot see this team's current quota.
