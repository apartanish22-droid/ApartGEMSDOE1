## Non-negotiable validation if a lane is supplied

Use the shared cached features and the attributed template's evaluator and writer, not a private fork. Freeze an independently reproducible whole-segment, spatially blocked hide-and-recover split with a buffer, derive all catalogue-dependent features and placements from visible faults only, and mask visible faults pixel-exactly. Test each feature separately; AUC above 0.90 is a leakage stop. Compare the candidate with a frozen current-best control under the *same* evaluator and withheld positives, and report pooled HOLDOUT-DTI, its evaluator version, sample count and 95% spatial-block confidence interval. Check the surface before placement and final dots against every applicable registry raster. Any absolute rank correlation above 0.90 or directional 3-pixel overlap above 70% is a duplicate stop. A format pass does not authorize a slot; selection is a separate step. At most three experiments or two hours.

The hidden expert test set is not the catalogue proxy. None of these mechanisms establishes a geothermal vent, and improvement on the proxy would not be a calibrated forecast of leaderboard performance.
