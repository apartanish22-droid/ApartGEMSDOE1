Pre-registration shortlist · not results

Candidate hypotheses for faults missing from the catalogue

These are planning candidates, not validated models or score projections. The current request does not include the designated single-method lane paragraph. The competition feature stack, labels, shared evaluator, and writer are absent from this checkout. Therefore no candidate is selected for implementation and no holdout was run.

Ranking is qualitative by expected scientific value per implementation cost. Exact layer names below are reported in the related owner-maintained GEMSDOE32 prepared-data manifest; that manifest is not an official organizer data dictionary and the corresponding raster is not present here. Validate the band tags against the official competition raster before use.

Rank / candidate Specific layers & signature Why it may find an unmapped fault How it differs from documented work Mimic, cost, and viability
1 · Signed potential-field phase pairing tmi / rtp magnetic anomalies and iso_grav_anom; scale-normalized signed curvature and paired zero-crossing/phase coherence, with cross-gradient orientation as a control. A deep basement discontinuity can perturb susceptibility and density beneath a surface with little topographic expression. Requiring compatible signed geometry across fields could reject single-field lithologic edges while retaining concealed fault boundaries. Prior owner-repository records include magnetic/gravity edge magnitudes, cross-gradient use, and Fourier upward continuation. This exact sign-sensitive, paired curvature/phase test is not shown as a completed result in the review material inspected. Novelty is narrow and must be checked against the shared experiment ledger before running. Named mimic: intrusive contacts and abrupt lithologic susceptibility/density changes. Expected improvement: moderate potential, low confidence; no numeric estimate. Cost: medium. It needs only the competition feature stack if these bands are present; that stack is missing here, so it is not currently testable.
2 · Fold-local conductivity residual corridors cond_surf, depth_to_base_surf, and elevation/relief channels; fit nuisance trends within each training fold, then detect directional residual ridges and coherent cross-scale persistence. Fluid-altered or fractured rock may leave a conductivity corridor that is not simply a proxy for relief or basin position. The fold-local residual can suppress broad regional trends and isolate off-catalogue structures. Related records use conductivity in fused surfaces, but the reviewed notes describe this fold-local nuisance residual as a proposal rather than a completed holdout result. Verify against the current shared ledger; do not assume untried status from a new name. Named mimic: saline playa brines, clay-rich basin fill, and alluvial aquifers. Expected improvement: low-to-moderate potential, mainly precision; no numeric estimate. Cost: medium. No new external data if the official competition bands are restored; unavailable in this checkout.
3 · Native-resolution scarplet-chain topology Competition-linked 1 m DEM tiles or verified USGS 3DEP elevation; segment short scarplets and score aligned en-echelon chains, strike consistency, and bounded along-strike gaps before aggregation to the prediction grid. A low-throw fault can appear as a chain of weak, discontinuous scarps that vanishes after coarse aggregation, even when the whole chain is structurally coherent. Earlier records describe 100 m scarp/relief derivatives and gap-closure methods; native-resolution segmentation and chain topology is a different scale and representation, not a re-labeling of a 100 m ridge score. Named mimic: Lake Lahontan shorelines, roads, terrace risers, and lithologic benches. Expected improvement: moderate only where surface expression exists; no numeric estimate. Cost: high storage/compute. Official source candidate: USGS 3DEP. Its coverage, tile access, and obtainability were not checked from this sandbox; therefore this is not viable yet.
4 · Relocated microseismicity plane fits Raw event coordinates, depth, and uncertainty from the official USGS FDSN event service; fit uncertainty-aware local planes and project their traces rather than using only gridded event-density fields. A buried active fault may produce a depth-coherent event plane with no mapped surface trace. Point-level geometry can preserve information lost by smoothing. Related submissions have tested seismicity/strain-density features; this exact relocated point-cloud plane fit is different, but the seismicity family is not wholly novel and deserves a low priority until the ledger is checked. Named mimic: mining blasts, induced events, and diffuse swarms. Expected improvement: potentially useful in active corridors but likely sparse; no numeric estimate. Cost: high. Official source candidate: USGS FDSN Event API. Host access, spatial coverage, and event quality were not checked here; therefore this is not viable yet.

Decision on the top-ranked candidate

Do not implement or spend a submission slot now. The top idea cannot be tested here because the required bands and labels are not in the checkout, the shared evaluator/writer are not in this repository, and the method lane is unspecified. This is an execution blocker, not a negative geological result. No new HOLDOUT-DTI value or 95% CI exists for this branch.

When the assigned lane and data are available: freeze the hypothesis; test each input feature alone as a leakage canary; derive all catalogue-based features from only visible segments in every fold; mask visible faults pixel-exactly; evaluate pooled DTI with the specified 300 m triangular kernel; compare against the same-fold incumbent; and check surface uniqueness before placement and dot overlap after placement. Stop at the declared experiment/time budget.

External-data availability rule

USGS 3DEP and the USGS FDSN service are named as official candidate sources, but their endpoints were not accessed in this environment. They are listed for future review only; neither is represented as reachable, complete, or suitable for this study area. No candidate needing them is called viable until coverage and a free, reproducible download are verified and recorded.
