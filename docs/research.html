GEMS Prize / Science & method Snapshot · 27 Oct 2026 UTC
SCIENCE / PREREGISTRATION

A physical hypothesis, not a renamed raster.

Fault mapping supports geothermal exploration. It does not itself establish a vent or a viable reservoir.

AP1-SNRC: the tested mechanism

Paired local potassium-versus-thorium rank residuals are sampled across the LiDAR strike normal, with a scarp step, signed-curvature amplitudes and a soft RTP-gradient corroborator. Quantized byte values are descriptors, not physical concentrations. No registry prediction or known-fault proximity is a field input.

Named mimics: alluvial material contacts, fluvial terraces, paleolake shorelines and mine cuts. Source coverage and survey boundaries can also create apparent edges; unavailable neighborhoods are excluded without labels.

Measured outcome

Candidate HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0456 · 95% CI 0.0393–0.0524; ablation HOLDOUT-DTI 0.0481 · 95% CI 0.0414–0.0549. Both use gems-segment-pooled-v1 and 60,988 withheld positives. Paired delta -0.00249, 95% CI [-0.00527, +0.00023]; 0 of 4 fold wins. There is no evidence of improvement.

All 18 individual source/descriptor canaries passed the 0.90 two-polarity AUC threshold. Passing a canary does not prove independence, fault discovery, or high performance.

Full preregistration, differences, mimics & data requirements ↓

Validation without hidden-label leakage

The shared template is copied byte-for-byte with source pins and checksum tests. Whole 8-connected fault components touching each quadrant/collar are hidden everywhere; features consume no fault catalogue. Training masks exclude the withheld regions and whole components with a buffer. Visible faults are masked pixel-exactly during evaluation. Connected components are a conservative raster proxy, not actual fault-segment IDs.

TPw, FPw and FNw are pooled, not fold-average ratios. The conditional 95% CI resamples 10 km spatial blocks. It quantifies internal validation uncertainty, not hidden-leaderboard uncertainty. A registry check occurs before placement and again on all final dots. No lattice-control exemption or registry-conditioned placement is used.
