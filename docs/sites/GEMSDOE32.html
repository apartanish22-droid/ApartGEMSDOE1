Why the B2 pruning could help

Measured identity: H33-B2 is exactly D2.8 after removing 6,436 dots at distance ≤200 m from known faults. There are zero newly added locations. The organizer masks only known-fault pixels themselves, not this surrounding band.

For fixed truth, DTI = TPw / (TPw + 0.2 FPw + 0.8 FNw). If pruning leaves TPw unchanged, FNw also stays fixed and reducing FPw increases DTI. But if real new faults lie near known traces, pruning can remove useful coverage. The official staff explicitly confirms that such new-fault pixels can occur.

Thus emission geometry and catalogue-flank false-positive removal are a plausible explanation of the family’s reported improvement. The exact hidden-test cause and the historical score-to-file pairing remain unverified. It does not establish that a new model will beat it.
