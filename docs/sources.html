Irregularities

Do not confuse a file check with a score.

The owner mirror's sample has positive pixels exactly where its label mirror does, whereas the public problem description describes an all-absence example. This is a discrepancy in the mirror, not proof that the official sample is wrong. Its finite mask is used for this audit with that limitation.

The previously reported portal range error could have several causes. The local validator checks inside-footprint NaN, infinity, masked values, sentinels and the [0,1] range, and separately reports all-finite and null-outside profiles. The official text mentions null/NaN outside bounds; handling of the irregular footprint and portal behavior remain unconfirmed.

The GEMSDOE32 wrapper converts positive probabilities to a Boolean mask; its contract has been reported upstream. A pinned upstream 57GEMSDOE writer preserved a synthetic 0.25 in a round-trip and passed 24 synthetic checks; this does not prove portal acceptance, holdout quality, or that the wrapper is fixed. No private evaluator or writer was used.

GitHub source heads are checked separately and moving heads are marked stale. We do not implement a DrivenData scraping or leaderboard-monitoring feed: its Terms of Use restrict automated and manual monitoring or copying without consent.
