What scores and audit gates do—and do not—mean

  • The 0.2778 for GEMSDOE32 H33-B2 is a user/owner-reported historical claim. No submission-page receipt ties it to the pinned TIFF hash. It is not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED and is not a justified score estimate.
  • The pixel re-audit confirms GEMSDOE32's 37,654-pixel support is identical to GEMSDOE25's support after removing cells within two pixels of the mapped-fault catalogue; GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical in-footprint support. This proves geometry, not why a claimed score occurred.
  • The repo's 3-pixel overlap threshold is an internal novelty protocol, not an official contest rule. Official rules separately contain an originality warranty. Passing the internal geometry gate would not itself establish compliance with that warranty.
  • The official max-kernel DTI can give nearby redundant predictions no extra maximum credit to a truth pixel while predictions outside its kernel contribute false-positive mass. Removing redundancy could help or could remove useful coverage. Without hidden labels and a receipt-linked controlled comparison, the causal explanation is unverified.
  • The historical HOLDOUT-DTI result is local mapped-catalogue hide-and-recover, not a calibrated estimate or confidence interval for the organizer's new expert-labeled faults. The current lane stopped before a v3 model holdout; do not present a projection as a score.
  • SHA-256 pinning establishes byte identity with an immutable owner repository revision; it does not establish organizer origin, correct preprocessing, license, complete registry coverage, or absence of hidden-label contamination.
  • Format mismatch: the prior raster has zeros outside the bridge footprint. The official text says outside-bounds data must be null/NaN, and the mirrored sample contains NaN outside its finite footprint. A locally constructed NaN-outside diagnostic variant with unchanged in-footprint predictions reproduces the sample's NaN mask, but it was not uploaded and portal acceptance is unknown. The strict all-finite/zero-outside writer is not cleared for submission.
  • The user-reported portal error “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]” cannot be tied to a file hash here. The prior TIFF is finite and in range under the local bridge check. Do not infer that NaNs caused the earlier portal error.
  • Local scripts can validate saved bytes, grid, values, holdout masks, and available pinned witnesses. They cannot access hidden truth, authenticate the organizer account, inspect the current quota, upload a file, or produce a submission receipt. A single duplicate witness is enough to stop this candidate; it is not a complete registry certificate.
