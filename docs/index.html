THE SCIENCE

Why did the
GEMSDOE32 family lead?

We can verify the construction, not the hidden causal story. The pinned GEMSDOE32 support equals the GEMSDOE25 dot set after removing all 6,436 dots within 200 m of the visible catalogue; the comparison has zero differing support pixels. The GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 dot sets are also identical.

The official metric uses maximum kernel credit per truth pixel, but accumulates false-positive mass across predictions. Thinning redundant dots and removing unhelpful near-catalogue predictions could improve that trade-off. Hidden labels are unavailable, so the observed score change does not prove this mechanism.
