Forensic interpretation

Why the cited GEMSDOE32 H33/B2 result may have ranked well

The most plausible explanation in the related archive is sparse thinning/pruning of a shared H19-5-derived spatial field, not proof that the H33/B2 model discovered more geothermal vents. Removing low-marginal-credit dots can reduce false-positive mass while preserving useful spatial coverage under an asymmetric Tversky-style objective. That is a hypothesis about scoring behavior, not verified causality.

  • Hash-verified raster-mask comparison: the selected GEMSDOE32 H33/B2 dots are a strict subset of GEMSDOE25 D2.8 and GEMSDOE31 solo masks.
  • The selected GEMSDOE36 map contains all GEMSDOE32 dots plus additional dots; the two maps are not independent methods.
  • GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical positive-pixel masks despite different GeoTIFF byte hashes.
  • No organizer receipt in this checkout binds the cited historical score to those exact bytes. Do not treat it as ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.
  • The archived -nan versions have NaNs outside the footprint; their allfinite companions have the same dot masks. That could trigger a strict full-array range check, but the exact portal error cause is unconfirmed without the failed file hash.
